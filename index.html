The research

Abstract

Transferring a large vision-language model into a compact image classifier is particularly useful when labeled examples are scarce. But learning from labels and teacher predictions through a single head can create conflicting optimization signals.

DHO gives these objectives separate prediction heads over a shared feature extractor. One learns from labeled examples; the other distills the teacher’s predictions. Their outputs are linearly combined at inference. The paper studies gradient alignment and representation quality, and evaluates the approach across 11 recognition datasets, low-label ImageNet settings, and out-of-distribution benchmarks.

Method

Separate the objectives. Share the representation.

A single-stage training procedure with two complementary sources of supervision.

01

Distill the teacher

A frozen vision-language model supplies soft predictions for the training images.

02

Learn with two heads

The supervised head learns from labels; the distillation head learns from the teacher.

03

Combine at inference

Interpolate the two predictions to use both task-specific learning and transferred knowledge.
